Privacy Policy — GeoClass

Last updated: 2026-11-11. The app is operated by GIS GEORGIA (github.com/GIS-GEORGIA/geoclass).

This policy describes how GeoClass handles data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Law of Georgia on Personal Data Protection. The app is built so that students stay unknown: we never ask for a name, and answers are linked only by a random code.

Who handles the data

The controller is GIS GEORGIA, contact: aigroegsig@gmail.com. Sessions are created and run by the teacher, who decides which game the class plays and when to delete the answers.

What we store

  • Student answers: the point placed on the map, the line or polygon drawn and what kind of place it is, the answers typed or chosen in a form, the score and the time sent. They are stored on the server and seen by the teacher, and by the class when shown on the class map.
  • A random identifier: made on the device, it links one student's answers together. The leaderboard nickname, such as "Blue Fox", is worked out from it.
  • Technical logs: records of server requests (time, address, response code and technical details, which may include the IP address) for finding faults. Cloudflare keeps them for no more than 7 days. To limit floods of requests the IP address is used for a moment and is not stored in our database.

The device keeps the language and theme choice, game progress and answers not sent yet. In the cards game the Android app can read the phone's compass to turn a card toward north. That reading is used on the device only and is never stored or sent. A teacher's device keeps the teacher key and the QR code links.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask students for a name, email, phone number or account.
  • We collect no health data, home address, ID number or photo. The app does not use the device's location (GPS), camera or microphone.
  • We don't use advertising or analytics services.
  • We don't sell data or share it for advertising.

The travel game and the coordinate detective (the name of the place) have a free text field. Please don't write personal information there. The teacher can hide or delete such an answer.

In the colour plates a student's phone only receives the plate the teacher shows. Students say their answers aloud; nothing is sent from the phone or stored. The session keeps only the list of plates the teacher chose. It is not a medical test.

In the map puzzle the session keeps which piece a student put in its place or in a wrong one, and when, as in the cards game. The phone builds the map picture directly from OpenStreetMap or OpenTopoMap tiles.

Why, and on what basis

We use the data only to make the classroom game work: to get the answer to the teacher, show it on the map and the leaderboard, and protect the service from abuse. The basis is our legitimate interest in providing an educational game (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We balance it by not collecting identities, keeping data only briefly and letting you delete it at any time. We don't use data for advertising, profiling or automated decisions.

Children

GeoClass is for school students. Since we ask for no name, contact or other identifying details, we don't know who a student is. We ask teachers to tell parents that the class uses GeoClass where school rules require it.

Third parties

  • Cloudflare — server and database. It processes data on our behalf under a data processing agreement (DPA). Cloudflare has servers in many countries, including the USA; transfers outside the EU rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • OpenStreetMap and OpenTopoMap — map tiles. Downloading them sends the device's IP address to their servers.
  • Google Maps — only if you press that button on the result page. The point opens in a separate app or browser.

How long we keep it

  • A session and all its answers are deleted automatically 6 months after it was made. The teacher can delete an answer, reset or delete a session sooner.
  • Technical logs: no more than 7 days.
  • A backup file the teacher saves on their own computer is in the teacher's hands. If an old backup is restored to the server, sessions older than 6 months are deleted again the next night.

Your rights

You have the right to ask to see, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to get a copy of it.

  • Delete it yourself: on the student page, the ⓘ button opens "Delete my answers". It deletes all answers sent from that device in that session, on the server and on the device.
  • Anything else: ask the teacher or write to us at aigroegsig@gmail.com. Because we store no names, please tell us the session name and roughly what the answer said. We reply within one month.
  • Complaints: you can turn to the Personal Data Protection Service of Georgia (personaldata.ge) or, if you live in the EU, to your country's data protection authority.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). The teacher key and the sessions' QR codes are stored on the server only as hashes. A student cannot see another student's data.

Cookies and browser storage

GeoClass uses no cookies. Browser storage holds only what the app needs to work (language, theme, game progress, answers not sent yet, the random code), so no consent banner is needed. You can clear it in the browser's settings.
